Ancient Birthright - Immortals Rebalance
========================================

Changes ONLY the Ancient Birthright `valyrian_immortals` Men-at-Arms type.

New stats
---------
Damage:          60
Toughness:       90
Pursuit:         10 (unchanged)
Screen:          25 (unchanged)
Stack size:      80
Max regiments:   1

Counters
--------
Pikemen:         1
Archers:         1
Heavy Infantry:  1
Heavy Cavalry:   2

Terrain and winter modifiers are copied from the Valyrian MAA mod's
Veterans of the Laurel Crown.

Everything else (recruitment condition, price, maintenance, provision cost,
AI quality, icon and unit type) remains as in Ancient Birthright.

Installation
------------
Copy BOTH:
  ab_immortals_rebalance.mod
  ab_immortals_rebalance/

to:
  Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/

Enable it and place it AFTER Ancient Birthright in the playset.

Compatibility
-------------
This mod deliberately overrides only the `valyrian_immortals` database entry.
Any later-loaded mod that also redefines that exact Men-at-Arms type will win.
