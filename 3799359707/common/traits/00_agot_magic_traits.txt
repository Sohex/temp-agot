# AGOT: Magic of Westeros - Traits
#
# Note: the "Awakened" gateway/tradition markers are NOT traits - they are
# character variables (see 00_agot_magic_effects.txt), because CK3 traits
# have no way to be hidden from the normal character trait bar (only
# shown_in_ruler_designer/shown_in_encyclopedia exist, which don't affect
# the portrait trait list). A visible trait would out every mage to anyone
# looking at their character sheet, which defeats the point of secret magic.
# Only the visible "corruption" traits below are real traits, since those
# are meant to be seen - they're the price of overusing a tradition.

### Visible corruption traits ###
# Earned through overuse of a tradition (see scripted_effects). Each carries
# a real, lore-flavored downside plus a small compensating power - magic
# always has a price, but the price also marks you as more deeply touched.
